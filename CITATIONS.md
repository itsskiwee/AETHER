# Data and method citations

## Data products

- Green, R. O., et al. *EMIT L2B Estimated Methane Plume Complexes 60 m V002*. NASA Land Processes Distributed Active Archive Center. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5067/EMIT/EMITL2BCH4PLM.002>. Cite the product version and pinned granule(s) used in each analysis.
- NASA Open Data Portal. *EMIT L2B Estimated Methane Plume Complexes 60 m V002* product description: <https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/emit-l2b-estimated-methane-plume-complexes-60-m-v002>. It describes the GeoJSON metadata fields and the `N/A` rate case when estimation criteria are not met.
- Green, R. O., et al. *EMIT L2B GHG User Guide*, Second Release, April 2025: <https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/2250/EMIT_L2B_GHG_User_Guide_V2.pdf>. Product file structure and metadata context; the guide does not establish the statistical interpretation of the native rate-uncertainty field mapped here.
- Carbon Mapper, Inc. *Data Platform*. Plume records are subject to the Carbon Mapper Terms of Use and require provider attribution. API documentation: <https://api.carbonmapper.org/api/v1/docs>. Terms: <https://carbonmapper.org/terms>.
